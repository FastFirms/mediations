## What information we collect

When you use the enquiry form on our website, we collect:

**Dispute information (non-identifying):**
- Type of dispute (e.g. parenting, property, workplace)
- Current stage of the dispute
- Preferred delivery method (online or in-person)
- Timing and urgency
- Fee preference category (e.g. standard, hardship, free services)
- Whether the other party is aware of or willing to participate in mediation

**Safety and contact preferences:**
- Whether you have any safety concerns relevant to contact (yes or no only)
- Which contact methods are safe for us to use (email, phone, voicemail)
- Preferred contact time

**Contact details:**
- Name
- Email address (if provided)
- Phone number (if provided)
- A brief description of your situation (optional free text)

**Technical information:**
- Your IP address (used for spam prevention; not retained in enquiry records)
- Page visited and source attribution (e.g. how you found us)

---

## How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:
- Route your enquiry to the appropriate intake pathway
- Contact you using the method and timing you specified
- Assess whether mediation may be appropriate for your situation
- Manage our intake and scheduling process

We do not use your information for marketing without your separate consent.

---

## Who can access your information

Your enquiry is stored securely in our case management system. Access is limited to:
- Authorised Mediations Australia staff involved in intake and mediation coordination
- Senior practitioners where escalation or professional review is required

Your contact details and free-text message are stored separately from your dispute categorisation and are accessible only to staff with appropriate authorisation.

**We do not share your information with:**
- Third parties for marketing purposes
- Other parties to your dispute without your explicit consent
- Any AI system for autonomous decision-making about your matter

**Third-party services used:**
- **Neon (database):** Enquiry records are stored in a managed PostgreSQL database hosted on AWS in the ap-southeast-2 region. Review Neon's privacy policy at [neon.tech].
- **Formspree:** A minimal notification (reference ID and queue status only — no contact details) is sent to our staff via Formspree. Review Formspree's privacy policy at [formspree.io].
- **Vercel:** Our website is hosted on Vercel. Review Vercel's privacy policy at [vercel.com].
