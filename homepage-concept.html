Why mediation

Strong outcomes, lasting resolution

Mediation gives both parties control over their outcome — unlike court where a judge decides. Most matters resolve in one to three sessions.

Typical mediation costs $2,500–$6,500 for both parties. A contested family court matter often exceeds $80,000 per side. The difference is your financial future.
Family Court proceedings average 14–24 months. Mediation can begin within weeks and most matters are resolved in one to three sessions.
Mediation is completely private. Court proceedings are on the public record. Everything said in mediation stays in mediation.

How it works

Resolution in three steps

01

Book a free consultation

Speak with a mediator at no charge. We'll assess your situation, explain the process, and answer every question. Appointments usually within days.

02

Attend a joint session

Both parties meet with the mediator — online or in person. We facilitate respectful dialogue, keep focus on outcomes, and help you explore options.

03

Reach a binding agreement

When you agree, we document the terms. For parenting and property matters, agreements become legally binding consent orders — without a courtroom.
