Mediation vs engaging a family lawyer in Townsville: the honest comparison

  • Cost. Mediation typically costs $2,000–$8,000 total (shared). Family law litigation commonly costs each party $15,000–$150,000 or more. Legal costs in family law are almost never recovered from the other side.
  • Time. Most Townsville mediations resolve in weeks. A contested matter through the Townsville registry of the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia can take one to three years.
  • Control. In mediation, you design the outcome. In court, a judge who has known your situation for hours decides matters you’ve lived for years.
  • Privacy. Mediation is confidential and conducted on a “without prejudice” basis. Court proceedings are public record.
  • Co-parenting. Litigation is adversarial. Mediation is collaborative — which matters when you will continue to co-parent or move in the same community.

Townsville family lawyers — common questions

Do I need a family lawyer in Townsville to separate?+

No. Separation requires no formal legal process. For the practical matters that follow — parenting and property — most Townsville couples resolve these through mediation without either party engaging a solicitor. Independent legal advice is worth getting at some stage, but you don’t need a family lawyer to begin.

What does a family lawyer in Townsville cost?+

Family lawyers in Townsville typically charge $300–$600 per hour. A contested matter through the Townsville registry of the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia commonly costs each party $15,000–$150,000 or more. Mediation typically costs $2,000–$8,000 total, shared between both parties.

Can I use a mediator instead of a family lawyer in Townsville?+

For the majority of Townsville family matters, yes — and it’s usually faster, cheaper and less stressful. Mediation resolves parenting arrangements, property and financial matters without court. Where you want legal advice, a lawyer can work alongside your mediator.

How long does mediation take compared to family court in Townsville?+

Most mediations resolve in one to two sessions, within weeks. A contested matter through the Townsville registry of the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia can take one to three years. The time difference is one of the most compelling reasons to try mediation first.

What if my ex has already engaged a family lawyer?+

It is still worth raising mediation. Many family lawyers actively encourage clients to attempt resolution before court — it is legally required for parenting matters, and it saves everyone time and money. We are happy to work with both parties and their lawyers.
