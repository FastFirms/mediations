Why choose mediation in Brisbane?

When a relationship breaks down — whether between separating partners, business owners, employer and employee, or family members — the instinct is often to engage lawyers and prepare for court. But for the overwhelming majority of Brisbane disputes, litigation is the slowest, most expensive and most stressful path available. Queensland actively promotes alternative dispute resolution across family, civil and commercial matters, and Brisbane's courts expect parties to attempt resolution before litigation. With South East Queensland's population growing rapidly, court lists are under pressure, making mediation both faster and more certain than waiting for a contested hearing.

Mediation offers a fundamentally different approach. Instead of handing control to a judge, you and the other party work with a neutral, nationally accredited mediator to reach your own agreement. The mediator doesn't take sides or impose a decision — their role is to guide a structured, confidential conversation that cuts through conflict and finds workable solutions. Sometimes court is genuinely necessary, and a good lawyer will tell you when. But trying mediation first almost always saves time, money and stress, whatever happens next.

Brisbane mediation vs going to court: an honest comparison

It's worth being clear-eyed about the difference between the two paths, because for most people in Brisbane the contrast is stark:

  • Time. Most Brisbane mediations resolve in a single day. A contested matter through the Brisbane registry at the Commonwealth Law Courts can take one to three years to reach a final hearing.
  • Cost. Mediation starts with a fixed fee and is usually shared between the parties. Litigation can consume tens or even hundreds of thousands of dollars per side — frequently a significant share of the very assets in dispute.
  • Control. In mediation you design the outcome. In court, a judge who has known your situation for hours decides matters you've lived for years.
  • Privacy. Mediation is confidential and conducted on a "without prejudice" basis. Court proceedings are part of the public record.
  • Relationships. Litigation is adversarial by design. Mediation is collaborative, which matters enormously when you'll continue to co-parent, share a business, or move in the same circles.

How the Brisbane court system fits in

Brisbane has a family law registry of the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia at the Commonwealth Law Courts on North Quay. The Brisbane registry circuits to Bundaberg, Hervey Bay, Maroochydore and Toowoomba, serving South East Queensland. Queensland's civil procedure rules encourage early dispute resolution, and family dispute resolution is generally required before parenting applications. Understanding where your matter would otherwise be heard matters, because it shows just how much mediation can save you. Rather than waiting for a listing at the Brisbane registry at the Commonwealth Law Courts, you can often resolve your dispute in a single day — and if you do reach agreement, we can formalise it so it carries the same legal weight as a court order.

2025 family law changes: what they mean for Brisbane clients

The Family Law Amendment Act 2024 made significant changes that took effect in May 2025. They affect how property settlements and parenting arrangements are assessed — including, critically, the consent orders that formalise mediated agreements.

  • Property settlements: Following the High Court's Ascot Investments decision, the controversial "add-backs" practice has largely been removed. Courts can no longer routinely add back amounts one party spent before trial, which changes the negotiating landscape — and makes reaching agreement through mediation even more valuable, because you control the outcome rather than waiting for a court to apply evolving law to your circumstances.
  • Parenting arrangements: The 2023 amendments (already in effect) removed the presumption of equal shared parental responsibility. Courts now focus purely on what is in the child's best interests, without a default presumption toward equal time. An agreed parenting plan reached in mediation gives both parents far more control over the outcome than any court order — and reflects what actually works for the family rather than a legal default.
  • Family violence emphasis: The amended Act gives greater weight to family violence considerations in both property and parenting matters. Where there are safety concerns, special mediation arrangements — including shuttle mediation, where parties never meet — are available. Call us to discuss this confidentially.

These changes make the case for early mediation in Brisbane stronger than ever. In a legal landscape that is actively evolving, an agreed outcome now is more predictable than waiting for a judge to apply new law to your circumstances — possibly 12–24 months from now.

Brisbane mediation FAQs

How much does mediation cost in Brisbane?+

In Brisbane you start with a free initial consultation, so there are no billing surprises. Mediation typically costs a fraction of a contested court case — often the difference between a few hundred to a few thousand dollars shared between the parties, versus tens of thousands per side in litigation. Most matters resolve in one or two sessions.

Where do you provide mediation in Brisbane?+

We serve the Brisbane CBD, Greater Brisbane, Ipswich, Logan, Redlands, the bayside suburbs and the western corridor. Our Brisbane office is located at Suite 507, 12B Anzac Square Arcade, 198 Adelaide St, Brisbane QLD 4000, and we also offer secure online mediation across QLD.

Do Brisbane family law matters have to go to court?+

Usually not. For most parenting matters you must attempt family dispute resolution first, and around 90% of the disputes we mediate resolve without a judge. A matter would only proceed to the Brisbane registry at the Commonwealth Law Courts if it genuinely cannot be resolved by agreement.

How long does mediation take in Brisbane?+

Most Brisbane mediations resolve within one to two sessions, often in a single day. By contrast, a contested matter through the Brisbane registry at the Commonwealth Law Courts can take one to three years to reach a final hearing.

Can I do Brisbane mediation online?+

Yes. We offer secure online mediation to all Brisbane and QLD clients, which is especially convenient for regional areas and removes any need to travel. Online agreements are just as binding once formalised.

Are your mediators nationally accredited?+

Yes. Our mediators are accredited under the Australian Mediator and Dispute Resolution Accreditation Standards (AMDRAS), the national benchmark, and many are also experienced family lawyers.

What types of disputes can be mediated in Brisbane?+

We mediate family and separation matters, property settlements, parenting arrangements, workplace and employment disputes, commercial and business disputes, estate and inheritance disputes, and personal injury claims across Brisbane and QLD.
