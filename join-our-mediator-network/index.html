Join the Mediations Australia Mediator Network

We're growing our national network of experienced mediators to help more Australians resolve disputes through a professional, impartial and constructive process. If you're an established practitioner who shares our commitment to quality dispute resolution, we'd welcome an expression of interest.

Express Your Interest ↓ Learn About Mediations Australia

Expressions of interest are reviewed individually. Submission does not guarantee appointment to the network.

Who we are

A national approach to resolving disputes

Mediations Australia helps individuals, families, organisations and professional advisers explore constructive alternatives to prolonged disputes and litigation. Our work spans a range of mediation needs, from family and property matters to workplace, commercial and estate disputes.

As demand for local and online mediation grows, we're developing a network of practitioners who can bring relevant experience, professional judgement and a commitment to impartial practice to matters across Australia.

Why express interest?

Be part of a professional, nationally connected network

Relevant matters

Opportunities to be considered for matters that align with your experience and practice areas.

Local and online capability

Help parties access suitable mediation, including face-to-face services where practicable.

Professional standards

A focus on competence, impartiality, confidentiality and appropriate professional practice.

Coordinated intake

A central team working toward clear enquiry handling, matter preparation and communication.

Who we'd like to hear from

Experienced mediators across Australia

We welcome expressions of interest from suitably qualified and experienced practitioners, including mediators with recognised Australian accreditation and practitioners with relevant specialist credentials. We are particularly interested in mediators who can support local, face-to-face matters as well as online mediation.

How it works

A straightforward first step

  1. Tell us about your practice. Complete the short form below with your credentials, experience, areas of practice and locations.
  2. Our team reviews your information. We may contact you to discuss experience, availability, professional requirements and potential fit.
  3. Next steps by invitation. Where appropriate, we may request verification documents or arrange a conversation before considering network participation.

Important: An expression of interest is not an application for employment and does not create a practitioner appointment or promise work.

Common questions

Questions from practitioners.

Do I need to be based in a capital city?+

No. We're interested in practitioners in metropolitan and regional locations, especially those able to provide face-to-face mediation in their local area.

Can I express interest if I primarily mediate online?+

Yes. Tell us about your online and in-person capabilities so we can understand your practice.

What practice areas are relevant?+

We welcome a range of mediation experience, including family, workplace, commercial, property, estate and other dispute areas. Please select the areas in which you have appropriate expertise.

Does submitting an expression of interest mean I've joined the network?+

No. Each expression of interest is reviewed, and any next steps are discussed individually.

Will I need to provide accreditation or insurance details?+

We ask for a summary of your professional credentials initially. Supporting verification may be requested later through an appropriate secure process.
