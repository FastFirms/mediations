How workplace mediation works

A structured, confidential process — from referral to signed agreement, typically in two to four weeks.

01

Referral & intake

You refer the matter. We contact all parties, explain the process, and confirm confidentiality agreements in writing.

02

Individual pre-sessions

Each party meets privately with the mediator to outline their perspective. This shapes the agenda and reduces surprises.

03

Joint mediation session

Both parties meet together with the mediator. Structured conversation, not argument. Most disputes resolve here.

04

Written agreement

Outcomes are documented in a signed written agreement — practical, owned by the parties, and ready to implement.

The disputes we resolve

We work with HR teams, people & culture managers, and senior leadership across all industries.

Colleague conflict

Interpersonal breakdowns between staff, including bullying and harassment complaints before they become formal grievances.

Manager–employee disputes

Disputes over performance, communication, or management approach — resolved before they escalate.

Team restructures

Conflict following restructures, redundancy processes, or changes to reporting lines.

Return-to-work conflict

Disputes following leave, injury, or a workplace investigation — a well-known flashpoint.

Unfair dismissal risk

Mediation before termination to reduce Fair Work Commission exposure and document good-faith process.

Multi-party disputes

Group conflict involving teams or multiple parties, structured with individual intakes before joint sessions.
