How it works

From referral to signed agreement — typically in two to four weeks

A structured, confidential process that most employers can initiate with a single call.

01

Referral & intake

You refer the matter. We contact all parties, confirm the process, and establish confidentiality agreements in writing.

02

Individual pre-sessions

Each party meets privately with the mediator to share their perspective and shape the session agenda.

03

Joint mediation session

Both parties meet with the mediator. Structured conversation, not argument. Most disputes resolve here.

04

Written agreement

Outcomes are captured in a signed written agreement — practical, owned by both parties, ready to implement.
